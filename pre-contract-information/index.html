What you buy

Bilmecik Full Version is a one-time digital purchase. It unlocks all paid games in the app and every game added later with free updates. Find the Match, Fruit Basket, Let's Count and the Farm are open without a purchase. It is not a subscription, it does not renew and it asks for no further payment.

The full version is available for as long as the app is on Google Play and your device supports it. The purchase is linked to your Google account and works on phones and tablets signed in with the same account.

Compatibility

Bilmecik runs on phones and tablets with Android 8.0 or later and Google Play. The games work offline. Buying and restoring need an internet connection and Google Play. Google Play confirms that the full version is unlocked, and the purchase is linked to your Google account. This is the technical protection measure for the content.

Changes to the content

Games may be improved with updates. We do not remove purchased games of our own choice. If a game has to be removed or changed only because of a legal obligation, a court or authority decision, a third-party rights claim or a Google Play rule, we add a similar game suitable for the same age group, free of charge, within a reasonable time. This does not limit your statutory rights.

The price is the amount, including taxes, that Google Play shows in its payment window at the time of purchase. Google Play takes the payment, and your payment details never reach us.

Delivery

The games unlock in the app as soon as Google Play confirms the payment. The app shows the order number, and Google also sends a receipt by email.

The full version is digital content delivered immediately. Before buying, you confirm in the app that the games unlock right away and that the right of withdrawal therefore does not apply. Google Play's own refund policy also applies. This does not affect your statutory rights.
