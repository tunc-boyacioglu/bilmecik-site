Bilmecik Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

Who we are

Bilmecik is a game app for children aged 2 to 4, developed by Tunç Boyacıoğlu. This page explains what information the app uses.

In short

Bilmecik does not collect any information and does not send anything anywhere. There are no ads. There are no accounts and no sign-in. Your child is never asked for a name, photo, voice or any other information. The games work without the internet.

What the app keeps on your phone

The app keeps some information only on your phone. This is each child profile's animal picture and level (Tiny, Little or Big), the daily time limit and the time played that day, progress and difficulty in the games, the pictures your child colors and the albums in the games, the sticker book, today's game, the animals on the farm, settings such as sound and language, a note that the games are unlocked after a purchase, and the "Today's practice" summary in the parent area. The summary covers the last 7 days, and older days are deleted automatically.

A profile has no name, date of birth or photo of the child. This information never leaves your phone. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not included in your phone's cloud backup. We cannot see it.

Parent area

Settings, the time limit, purchases and links to websites are in the parent area, behind a lock that young children cannot pass. Links there open in your phone's browser or email app.

Purchases

Paid games are bought through Google Play. Google receives your payment details and handles them under Google's privacy policy. The app only reads the purchase confirmation from Google Play on your phone and unlocks the games. It keeps a note on your phone that the games are unlocked, so the app also opens without the internet. Your payment details never reach us.

The internet

The app does not request internet permission and does not connect to the internet. Purchases and restoring purchases go through the Google Play app on your phone.

We share nothing

The app has no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting tools. It contains no software from other companies that collects or sends information. The open-source libraries we use, such as the game engine, run only on your phone and send nothing. So there is no information for us to share.

If the app crashes, depending on your phone's settings, Google may collect technical crash information under its own rules (for example the phone model, the Android version and where the error happened) and show it to us in Play Console in aggregate. Google collects this, not the app, and we cannot identify you or your child from it.

Phone permissions

The app does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos. It does not use the advertising ID. The only permissions it asks for are for Google Play purchases and a few technical permissions these need.

If you email us

If you send us an email, we use your email address, your name and what you write only to answer you and solve your problem. The data controller is Tunç Boyacıoğlu. The legal basis is performing our contract with you and our legitimate interest in answering you (Article 5(2)(c) and (f) of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law, KVKK). Emails are kept on the servers of our email provider Natro in Türkiye and are not transferred abroad. We share them with no one else. We delete them within 6 months after your issue is solved. If your email is a complaint about a purchase, we may keep it as evidence until the legal limitation period ends. Please do not write information about your child in your email. To use your rights under Article 11 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) or under the data protection law of your country, write to the same address. We reply within 30 days.

Keeping information safe

Information on your phone stays in the app's own storage, and other apps cannot read it. When you delete the app, this information is deleted too.

Deleting

You can delete all information with "Reset data" in the parent area, by uninstalling the app, or by clearing the app's data in your phone settings. Your purchase stays linked to your Google account and comes back when you install the app again.

This website

This website has no cookies, no counters, no analytics and no fonts loaded from other websites. The hosting service may keep visitors' IP addresses in its own logs for security.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page and the date at the top is updated. If the app ever starts collecting information, this will be stated here clearly before that update is released.
